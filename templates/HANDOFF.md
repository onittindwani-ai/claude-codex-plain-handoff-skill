# Handoff Templates

Pick the form that fits. Keep the headings exactly as written and in this order. Replace everything in square brackets.

## Standard form (most tasks, 120 words or fewer)

```
**Heads up:** [Anything I did that you didn't ask for, anything that broke, anything I deleted, anything risky, any problem I noticed but didn't fix. Or: Nothing unexpected.]

**What I did:**
- [What changed for you or your customers, in 1 plain sentence]
- [Second change, if any]
- [Third change, if any]

**Does it work:** [Yes, I tested it by ... / Partly: ... works, ... doesn't yet / Not tested yet, because ...]

**Not done yet:** [What's left, or: Nothing, it's finished.]

**Your decision:** [1 question, with options A and B and my recommendation. Or: Nothing needed from you.]
```

## Short form (small tasks, 60 words or fewer)

```
**Heads up:** [Surprise or problem. Or: Nothing unexpected.]

**What I did:** [1 plain sentence.]

**Does it work:** [Yes, tested by ... / Not tested, because ...]

**Your decision:** [1 question, or: Nothing needed from you.]
```

## Failed form (the task didn't work)

```
**Heads up:** [What doesn't work, and what that means for you, in 1 plain sentence.]

**What I did:**
- [What I tried, in 1 line]
- [What I made sure is safe, for example: I kept it switched off so customers don't see it]

**Does it work:** No. [What I think the cause is. Say "I think" if not sure.]

**Not done yet:** [What it will take to fix it.]

**Your decision:** [The 1 simple thing I need from you, in 1 sentence, with how long it takes. Or 1 question with options and my recommendation.]
```

## Before starting work after a vague request

Use this when the person says something like "fix everything", "just make it work" or "do it all".

```
Here's what I'll do: [1 plain sentence].
[Only if needed:] One thing first: [a risky step, or an earlier surprise they never answered, and what I'll do about it].
Reply yes to go, or tell me what to change.
```

If the plan is small, safe and easy to undo, say what you'll do in 1 line and carry on in the same reply.

## When the person asks for "details" or "the technical version"

Give the full technical report in that reply only. Start with: "Here's the full technical version:" and end with: "Back to short summaries from the next task." Then return to plain handoffs.
